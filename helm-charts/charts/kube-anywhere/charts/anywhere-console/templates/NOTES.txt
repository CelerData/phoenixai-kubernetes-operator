Add, remove or rotate accounts with `kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} edit secret {{ template "anywhere.adminSecretName" . }}`;
changes take effect within about a minute, no restart needed.

The Cluster Console is a second sign-in page, at /cluster-console/login, for the people who query
a cluster rather than operate the fleet: select a cluster there, then use that cluster's own
PhoenixAI Database username and password. No Anywhere Console account is involved, and the two
consoles keep separate session cookies, so one browser can hold both sessions at once.
